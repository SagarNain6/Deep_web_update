The SPPL Approach

Shipbuilding Process

1. Design & Engg

Utilizing advanced CAD/CAM software and CFD analysis to optimize hull forms and stability.

2. Keel Laying

Precision block fabrication, sub-assemblies, and hull construction using automated CNC.

3. Outfitting

Integration of propulsion systems, navigation suites, electrical grids, and crew spaces.

4. Sea Trials

Rigorous harbor acceptance tests, sea trials, load testing, and class approvals.
