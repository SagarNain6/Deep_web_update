About SPPL

Pioneering Engineering Excellence in India and Beyond

Company Overview

Suryadipta Projects Pvt. Ltd. (SPPL) is a premier engineering corporation headquartered in India.

Established over two decades ago, SPPL has grown from a specialized fabrication unit into a massive conglomerate encompassing shipbuilding, offshore engineering, heavy structural manufacturing, and turnkey EPC projects. We are recognized by the Government of India and major classification societies globally for our uncompromising commitment to quality and safety.

Our sprawling manufacturing and shipyard facilities are equipped with state-of-the-art machinery, allowing us to execute complex projects for the Indian Navy, Coast Guard, Oil & Gas majors, and heavy industries worldwide.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized leader in heavy engineering and maritime manufacturing, driven by innovation, technological superiority, and sustainable practices, setting the benchmark for excellence in the industry.

Our Mission

To consistently deliver world-class products and solutions that exceed client expectations through engineering precision, strict adherence to quality and safety standards, and continuous capability enhancement.
